Onori Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026

The short version

Onori collects no data. We have no servers that hold your information, no analytics, no ads, no user accounts. Your financial data never reaches us.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in Onori, such as transactions, budgets, bills, savings goals and receipts, is stored on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, synced through your personal iCloud (CloudKit private database) under your Apple Account. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to this data.

What we collect

Nothing. Onori contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking. We cannot see your data, your identity, or your usage.

Optional features you control

Receipts: a receipt you scan is read on your device. Its photo is kept with your ledger, in the same places as the rest of your data.

Export: when you export your ledger, the file is created on your device and goes only where you send it. If you attach it in an assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT, it becomes content in that assistant's cloud, and that assistant's privacy policy applies.

Assistant access on Mac: if you connect a desktop assistant to Onori on your Mac, it can read what you allow and nothing else, and it cannot change your ledger. What it reads is sent to that assistant's AI provider and handled under its privacy policy.

Currencies: to convert between currencies the app downloads two public files: the day's exchange rates from onori.money, and the list of currency names and symbols from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev), a public exchange-rate service. Both files are the same for everyone, and the requests contain no personal or financial information. Like any web request, each one reaches its host from your IP address.

Household sharing

If you share your household with a family member, sharing happens through iCloud's own sharing mechanism between your Apple Accounts. We are not involved and cannot see shared data.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your payment details.

App Store

Apple may provide Onori with aggregated, anonymized statistics (downloads, sales, crashes) via App Store Connect. These contain no personal financial data.

This website

onori.money sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address. Questions: support@onori.money
