Home → Family

One budget for the household. No shared passwords.

Most couples manage money across two phones, three apps and a spreadsheet nobody updates. Onori gives both of you the same live ledger, while each of you keeps your own Apple Account, your own Face ID, your own phone.

How Family works

Shared household ledger

Invite your partner from the app. The ledger syncs through your own iCloud accounts with Apple's CloudKit sharing. There is no Onori server in the middle, ever.

Plus, shared with your family

Onori Plus supports Apple Family Sharing: buy it once, and the members of your Apple family group have Plus on their own devices.

Everyone edits, everyone sees

Both of you add purchases, plan the paycheck and pay the bills. One number and one always-current picture of the household money.

Built for real households

  • One budget from payday to payday, with one number both of you can check before buying
  • Bills visible to both, so nobody asks what that charge was
  • A purchase abroad lands in the shared budget in your main currency, converted from the same daily rates on both phones
  • Each person signs in with their own Apple Account: nothing to share, nothing to leak

Privacy, still

Family Sharing extends Plus; iCloud sharing extends the data, between your own Apple Accounts only. Onori still has no servers and still can't see a single transaction. How that works →
