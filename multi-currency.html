Home → Multi-currency

Many currencies. One honest number.

Paid in one currency, travelling in another, a subscription billed in a third: that is normal life for millions of households. Onori keeps one budget in your main currency and puts every purchase into it at the right rate.

How Onori handles currencies

One main currency

Your paycheck, envelopes, bills and the number you can spend a day are all in one currency, the one you choose as main. Nothing is summed across currencies behind your back.

Pay in any other

Enter a purchase in the currency you paid in. It is converted at the rate of its own day and lands in its envelope in your main currency, so an old trip does not shift when rates move.

Rates you can correct

Rates arrive automatically once a day. If your bank used a different one, set the rate for that currency yourself; it is used for totals until you switch back to automatic.

Built for real multi-currency life

  • Travelers. Dinner in euros, a taxi in dram, and the budget at home still adds up
  • Expats and remote workers. Earn in one currency, spend in the local one, and see what is left in the one you think in
  • Households. Both phones convert from the same daily file, so two people never see two different totals for the same ledger
  • More than 150 currencies. If you can pay in it, you can enter it

And still: no servers

The rates are one public file, the same for everyone, that the app downloads once a day. Your amounts never leave your devices and your own iCloud. Privacy by architecture →
