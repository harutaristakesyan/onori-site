# Onori press kit

Onori is a paycheck budget for iPhone and Mac that shows one number, what you can spend a day
until payday, and keeps the ledger in your own iCloud.

## Key facts

- Platform: iPhone (iOS 26+) and Mac (macOS 26+)
- Category: Finance
- Privacy: no data collected. The ledger is stored on your devices and in your own iCloud. An
  optional assistant connection sends what you permit to the AI provider you chose.
- Pricing: free to keep by hand. Onori Plus: $6.99 a month, $39.99 a year or $79.99 one-time.
  Assistant Access on Mac: $29.99 one-time.
- Signature feature: one number, what you can spend a day until payday, from a budget that runs
  from paycheck to paycheck.
- Family: a household ledger shared through iCloud between your own Apple Accounts. Inviting is
  part of Plus; joining is free. Plus supports Apple Family Sharing.
- Languages: English, German, Russian
- Developer: Harut Aristakesyan & family (Armenia)
- Website: https://onori.money
- Contact: support@onori.money

## The story

Onori is built by a husband-and-wife team based in Armenia. Harut Aristakesyan writes the code,
and the app runs the family's own budget every day. Onori exists because they wanted a budget
with two properties that rarely coexist, an answer you can use today and real privacy, and
couldn't find one.

Instead of connecting to banks through aggregators or bundling yet another paid chatbot, Onori
made a bet: your iPhone and your iCloud are enough to run your budget, and the assistant you
already use is enough to ask about it.
