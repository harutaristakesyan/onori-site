Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

The short version

Onori collects no data. We run no analytics, show no ads and have no user accounts. Your financial data never reaches us.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in Onori, such as transactions, budgets, bills, savings goals and receipts, is stored on your device and, when iCloud is on, synced through your own iCloud (your private CloudKit database) under your Apple Account. Apple handles iCloud data under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to this data.

What we collect

Nothing. Onori contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no tracking. We cannot see your data, your identity or how you use the app.

Features you control

Receipts. A receipt you scan is read on your device. Its photo is kept with your ledger, in the same places as the rest of your data.

Export. When you export your ledger, the file is created on your device and goes only where you send it.

Assistant access on Mac. This is optional. If you connect a desktop assistant to Onori on your Mac, it can read what you allow and nothing else, and it cannot change your ledger. What it reads is handled by that assistant under its own privacy policy.

Currencies. To convert between currencies the app downloads two public files: the day's exchange rates from onori.money, and the list of currency names and symbols from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev), a public exchange-rate service. Both files are the same for everyone, and the requests carry nothing about you or your ledger. Like any web request, each one reaches its host from your IP address.

Household sharing

If you share your ledger with a family member, sharing happens through iCloud's own sharing between your Apple Accounts. We are not involved and cannot see shared data.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your payment details.

App Store

Apple may give us aggregated, anonymized statistics, such as downloads, sales and crashes, through App Store Connect. They contain no personal financial data.

This website

onori.money sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address. Questions: support@onori.money
