  • Pay periods, not months

    The bills due before payday are set aside first. What is left is divided over the days that remain, today included.

  • Your ledger stays yours

    It lives on your devices and in your own iCloud. There is no account to create, and nothing is stored on a server of ours.

  • One household, one ledger

    Share it with your partner through iCloud. Each person uses their own Apple Account.
