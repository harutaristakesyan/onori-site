What’s new in Onori.

Every release, in plain words. Onori updates automatically from the App Store; each version keeps your data exactly where it was, on your devices and in your own iCloud.

1.0 — the first release{% unless site.app_store_url and site.app_store_url != "" %} · coming soon{% endunless %}

  • The number. What you can spend a day until payday, with the calculation behind it one tap away.
  • A plan for every paycheck. Bills due before payday are set aside first, envelopes share the rest, savings goals get their part.
  • Bills and savings goals. Pay a bill in one tap, even early; add to a goal and see the number adjust.
  • iPhone and Mac. One ledger on both, synced through your own iCloud, with a native Mac app.
  • Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts. Six Home Screen widgets, two Control Center buttons, and Siri answers for what is left and what you spent.
  • Onori Plus. Apple Pay purchases saved on their own, receipts read on your iPhone, bills marked as paid automatically, insights and a weekly digest, household sharing, multi-currency.
  • Assistant Access on Mac. A read-only connection for Claude or ChatGPT, as its own purchase.
  • English, German and Russian.
