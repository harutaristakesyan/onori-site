Your everyday spending, together.

Record purchases, follow the envelopes and see what is left until payday. Share a household ledger with your partner through your own iCloud accounts. Inviting is part of Onori Plus, and joining is free; see how sharing works.

On the Mac · optional

Ask about your money.

With Assistant Access, Claude or ChatGPT on your Mac can read your ledger through a local, read-only connection and answer questions about it.

Asked how much was spent since payday in the sample ledger, the right answer is $325.04: five purchases, not counting the bill that was paid. It is the same figure the Mac app shows on its Spent line.

You choose what
it can read.

Connect Claude or ChatGPT on your Mac from Onori's Assistants settings. The connection is read-only: an assistant cannot change your ledger, and Onori has to be open and unlocked while it reads.

Assistant Access is optional, a separate one-time purchase, and needs setup. What you permit is sent to the assistant's AI provider. Read the privacy policy.
