Simple, honest pricing

Keeping the budget by hand is free: no account, no card, no countdown. Onori Plus does the typing for you, and you can pay for it by the month, by the year or once. Cancel anytime in your iPhone Settings. And a promise: if you stop paying, nothing you entered is locked.

What is free

  • The number: what you can spend a day until payday, and the calculation behind it
  • Paychecks, envelopes, bills and savings goals, from payday to payday
  • Transactions added by hand, with categories that are remembered
  • Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, a lock with Face ID, CSV export
  • The Mac app, and sync between your devices through your own iCloud

What Plus adds

  • Apple Pay purchases, saved on their own
  • Receipts scanned and read on your iPhone
  • Bills marked as paid automatically
  • Insights and a weekly digest
  • Household sharing: inviting your partner into one ledger. Joining is free
  • Multi-currency: purchases in a currency other than your main one

The data promise

Your data is never held hostage. Cancel Plus and nothing locks. The budget keeps working by hand, everything you entered stays visible and yours, and CSV export stays free. We think that is the only honest way to run a paid finance app.

Common questions

Is there a free trial?

There is no trial because there is no need for one: the free app is not a countdown. Use it for as long as you like, and add Plus when you want Onori to do the typing.

Is Plus shared with my family?

Yes. Monthly, yearly and lifetime Plus all support Apple Family Sharing, so the members of your Apple family group get Plus on their own devices.

How do I cancel?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Onori → Cancel. Apple handles all billing; we never see your payment details.

Why is Assistant Access separate?

It is a different kind of feature, used on the Mac only and by far fewer people, so it is its own one-time purchase instead of making Plus cost more for everyone.
